# Product Definition — GHCA (Greenberg-Hastings Cellular Automata)

## Vision & Purpose
GHCA is an exploratory computational neuroscience and AI research repository investigating Greenberg–Hastings excitable dynamics on graphs as a substrate for reward-driven learning (E-series) and causal-inference instrumentation (C-series).

## Core Capabilities
- **E-Series (Excitable Learning Substrate):** Demonstrating working memory, selective attention, executive control, and emergent conjunction routing using local excitable media dynamics.
- **C-Series (Causal Instrumentation):** Measuring macro-sufficiency, outcome relativity, fat-handed interventions, and synthetic SCM benchmarks on excitable graphs.
- **Closed-Loop Substrate Plasticity (Current Focus):** Developing multi-axis local plasticity ($\tau$-adaptation for refractory timescales, $\theta$-adaptation for homeostatic excitability, $W_{\text{rec}}$ for Hebbian structural routing) to eliminate catastrophic forgetting and enable substrate-level credit assignment.

## Key Stakeholders & Impact
- **Computational Neuroscience & Neuromorphic AI Researchers:** Interested in non-von-Neumann, physical-substrate computation and temporal phase-field codes.
- **Causal Representation Learning (CRL) Community:** Seeking benchmarks for macro-sufficient variable discovery under degeneracy and fat-handed macro-interventions.
